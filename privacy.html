Eltanin Calendar — Privacy policy

Eltanin Calendar is used by one person, its owner S M A Nahian, so his own assistant can read his calendars.

  • Access: read-only Google Calendar access (calendar.readonly). It cannot create, edit or delete events, and it does not access Gmail, Drive or any other Google data.
  • Use: event data is used only to show the owner his schedule, find free time and remind him of events.
  • Storage: the access token and event data stay on the owner's own computer. Nothing is sold or shared with third parties.
  • Revoke: remove access anytime at myaccount.google.com/permissions.
